I am a Research Scientist at [NVIDIA Research](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/twn/), working on Embodied AI.

My research focuses on reasoning-enhanced vision-language-action models that enable robots to reason about tasks and environments and translate that reasoning into effective actions in the physical world.

Before joining NVIDIA as a Research Scientist, I received my Ph.D. from [National Taiwan University (NTU)](https://www.ntu.edu.tw/english/) in January 2026, advised by Prof. [Yu-Chiang Frank Wang](https://vllab.ee.ntu.edu.tw/ycwang.html). I also earned my bachelor's degree in [Computer Science and Information Engineering (CSIE)](https://www.csie.ntu.edu.tw/?locale=en) from NTU in 2022 and worked as an Applied Scientist Intern at Microsoft, developing deep learning models for Bing Maps.
